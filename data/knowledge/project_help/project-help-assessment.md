# Understanding a course assessment

A course risk assessment helps an advisor review what may need attention in an active course. The risk estimate concerns course failure or withdrawal. It supports advisor judgment and does not make an automatic academic decision.

The course explanation describes observed activity or submissions associated with the model result. It does not establish why a student is struggling or guarantee that a suggested action will improve the outcome. Advisors can review the record and discuss a useful next step with the student.

If a course assessment is unavailable, required information may be incomplete or the service may be unavailable. This does not mean the student has no activity or has failed. Refresh assessment retries the current assessment. Completed courses remain in historical enrollments and do not receive current risk estimates.

This Sprint 2 demonstration uses synthetic activity and a model trained on OULAD. It has not been validated on institutional AcadMate or Brightspace students. These project instructions are not university academic policy.
