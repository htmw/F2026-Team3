# Submitting and reviewing a degree plan

To submit a degree plan in AcadMate, open Degree plan, review your courses, and choose Send for approval on the term you want your advisor to review. The plan will show Pending approval after a successful submission.

If your advisor requests changes, read their feedback on Degree plan, revise the relevant courses, and send the plan for approval again. An approved plan shows Approved. Approval of course choices does not confirm course availability or registration.

Degree progress shows completed credits, courses in progress, and remaining requirements. The expected graduation date is an estimate with stated assumptions. For university graduation rules or repeat-course policy, ask your advisor and consult the applicable official catalog.
