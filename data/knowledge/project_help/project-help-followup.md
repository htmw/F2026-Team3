# Messages and advising meetings

To send a message in AcadMate, open Messages and choose an allowed contact. Use contact search to find a person who is not in the initial contact list. Advisors can also open a student record and choose Message to select that student. Review your message before sending it.

To book an advising meeting as a student, open Book a meeting, choose an allowed advisor and an available slot, then review the meeting details before submitting the request. An advisor can choose Schedule follow-up from a student record to open the meeting dialog with that student selected. Opening a dialog alone does not book a meeting; the resulting status shows whether a request is waiting or confirmed.

Students can use Messages to contact an allowed advisor and use the meeting booking page to choose an available time. Submitted messages and meeting requests use the existing advising workflow. This local demonstration does not verify external email or calendar delivery.
